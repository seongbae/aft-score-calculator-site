AFT Score Calculator

Support

AFT Score Calculator scores your Army Fitness Test events on your device using the published AFT scoring tables. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or scoring issues? Email bae.seong@hotmail.com. Include your device model and app version (shown on the app’s More page) if you’re reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where are my saved results stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so use Export on the More page to keep a backup.

How do I move results to a new phone? Export from the old device, send the file to the new one, then use Import on the More page.
